=== ICT & SMART MONEY CONCEPTS (SMC) 104-TOPIC INSTITUTIONAL TRADING PLAYBOOK ===

[FOUNDATIONS: TOPICS 1-14]
1. Market Structure: Swing Highs (HH/LH) aur Swing Lows (HL/LL) ka sequence. Bullish structure mein HLs defend hote hain; Bearish mein LHs defend hote hain.
2. Swing Points (STH/ITH/LTH): Short-Term High (3-candle fractal), Intermediate-Term High (dono taraf lower STHs), Long-Term High (major liquidity pool).
3. Break of Structure (BOS): Trend continuation. Candle body ka previous swing high (bullish) ya previous swing low (bearish) ke upar/neeche close hona.
4. Market Structure Shift (MSS / CHoCH): Trend reversal signal. Agla swing point opposite direction mein break hota hai violent displacement candle ke sath. Wick nahi, body close zaroori hai.
5. Premium vs Discount: Dealing range ka 50% midpoint. 50% ke upar Premium (sirf Sell dekho); 50% ke neeche Discount (sirf Buy dekho).
6. Equilibrium: Dealing range ka exact 50.0% level. Fair price zone.
7. Dealing Range: Current anchor swing low se swing high tak ki range jisme price trade kar rahi hai.
8. Internal vs External Range Liquidity: External = Swing Highs/Lows (BSL/SSL). Internal = Range ke andar ke PD Arrays (FVGs, OBs). Price hamesha External se Internal aur Internal se External travel karti hai.
9. Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL): Old highs, equal highs (EQH), ya trendline liquidity jahan buyers ke buy-stops aur short sellers ke stop losses baithe hain.
10. Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL): Old lows, equal lows (EQL) jahan institutional stop hunts target hote hain.
11. Equal Highs / Lows (EQH / EQL): Clean double tops ya double bottoms. Ye retail trap liquidity magnets hote hain. Smart money hamesha inko sweep karti hai.
12. Old Highs / Lows: Previous Day High (PDH), Previous Day Low (PDL), Previous Week High/Low (PWH/PWL).
13. Liquidity Sweep / Stop Hunt: Price wick banakar BSL ya SSL ke bahar jati hai, liquidity grab karti hai aur turant range ke andar wapas close hoti hai (False breakout / Turtle soup).
14. Higher Timeframe (HTF) Bias: 4H aur Daily structure supreme hai. 15m/5m par hamesha HTF bias ki direction mein trade lo.

[PD ARRAYS: TOPICS 15-34]
15. Bullish Order Block (OB): Down candle before a strong up move jo market structure break (MSS/BOS) karti hai aur FVG chhodti hai. Demand zone.
16. Bearish Order Block (OB): Up candle before a violent down move jo MSS/BOS karti hai. Supply zone.
17. Fair Value Gap (FVG): 3-candle imbalance. Candle 1 aur Candle 3 ke beech khali space.
18. BISI (Buyside Imbalance Sellside Inefficiency): Bullish FVG. Candle 1 High aur Candle 3 Low ke beech gap.
19. SIBI (Sellside Imbalance Buyside Inefficiency): Bearish FVG. Candle 1 Low aur Candle 3 High ke beech gap.
20. Inversion FVG (IFVG): Jab koi FVG price ko respect karne ki jagah aggressively break ho jata hai aur wapas aane par support/resistance flip ban jata hai.
21. Breaker Block: Failed order block. Ek aisa OB jo liquidity sweep ke baad MSS deta hai aur opposite direction mein entry deta hai.
22. Mitigation Block: Failure swing ke baad banne wala block jisme liquidity sweep nahi hui hoti lekin MSS hota hai.
23. Rejection Block: Candle ki long wicks jo liquidity sweep karti hain; wick area entry zone hota hai.
24. Propulsion Block: Active OB ke upar banne wala dusra OB jo trend ko accelerate karta hai.
25. Vacuum Block: Weekend ya news events par aane wale large price gaps.
26. Liquidity Void: Single directional extreme expansion jisme multiple candles bina wicks ke move karti hain. Price isko 100% fill karne wapas aati hai.
27. Volume Imbalance: Do consecutive candles ke open aur close ke beech ka micro gap jahan koi trading volume nahi hui.
28. Consequent Encroachment (CE): Kisi bhi FVG ya Liquidity Void ka exact 50.0% midpoint. Price CE ko touch karke reverse honi chahiye; CE ke paar body close weakness dikhata hai.
29. Optimal Trade Entry (OTE): Fibonacci retracement levels 62.0%, 70.5%, aur 79.0%. 70.5% sweet spot entry hoti hai.
30. Balanced Price Range (BPR): Do overlapping opposite FVGs (BISI aur SIBI) jo same price range mein bante hain aur perfect efficiency banate hain.
31. Order Block Mean Threshold: Order Block candle ka exact 50.0% midpoint. Mean threshold ke upar/neeche body close invalidation maani jaati hai.
32. Relative Equal Highs/Lows: Almost equal levels jo retail resistance/support dikhte hain, par actually liquidity pools hote hain.
33. Opening Gap: New Day ya New Session ka first candle opening gap.
34. Gap-Based PD Arrays: FVG, IFVG, BPR, NWOG, NDOG ka combined use.

[TIME & KILLZONES: TOPICS 35-58]

38. NY Lunch (12:00 - 13:00 NY / 21:30 - 22:30 IST): Low volume consolidation aur stop hunts. No trade zone.
39. NY PM Killzone (13:30 - 16:00 NY / 23:00 - 01:30 IST): Afternoon trend continuation ya late day reversal.
40. London Close Killzone (10:00 - 12:00 NY / 19:30 - 21:30 IST): London traders profit booking karte hain; minor counter-trend pullbacks.
41. Power of 3 (AMD): Accumulation (Asian Range) -> Manipulation (London Judas Sweep) -> Distribution (NY Trend Expansion).
42. Judas Swing: True direction se opposite banne wala fake move jo session open par liquidity grab karne ke liye banta hai.
43. Midnight Open (00:00 NY): Daily true open reference price. Bullish day par price midnight open ke neeche discount mein buy hoti hai.
44. 8:30 AM NY Open: US news releases aur morning algorithm injection point.
45. True Day Open: Session directional bias reference.
46. New Week Opening Gap (NWOG): Friday close aur Sunday open ke beech ka gap; strong magnetic support/resistance.
47. New Day Opening Gap (NDOG): Daily open gaps jo institutional targets hote hain.
48. Opening Range Gap (ORG): Market session open standard ranges.
49-51. Daily/Weekly/Monthly Profiles: Classic Tuesday Low/High of the week formation.
52-54. IPDA Lookback (20, 40, 60 Days): Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm lookback windows liquidity pools dhundne ke liye.
55-57. Daily/Weekly Bias & High/Low of Day: London open ya NY AM open par aksar din ka extreme ban jata hai.
58. Friday Seek & Destroy: Weekend se pehle dono taraf ke stops sweep karna.

[RISK & EXECUTION DISCIPLINE: TOPICS 93-104]
93. Risk Percentage: Maximum 1.0% to 2.0% risk per trade on account capital.
94. Risk-Reward (RR): Minimum 1:2.0 R:R required. Never take 1:1 or negative setups.
95. Position Sizing: Lot size = (Account Balance * Risk %) / (Stop Loss in Pips * Pip Value).
96. Partial Take Profit: 50% lot close at 1:2 or first liquidity pool; move SL to Break-Even.
97. Break-Even Rules: Price ne jab first internal range liquidity (opposite FVG) tap kar li, tabhi SL ko Entry par shift karo, pehle nahi.
98. Stop Loss Placement: FVG ke low/high ke bahar ya Order Block ke 50% Mean Threshold ke peeche.
99. Trade Management: Plan the trade, trade the plan. Do not touch trade during news volatility.
100. Journaling: Screenshot before entry, screenshot after exit, checklist adherence score.
101. Backtesting: Minimum 100 sample trades per setup before live deployment.
102. Psychology: FOMO avoid karo. Daily max 2 trades.
103. Daily Loss Limit: 2 consecutive losses (-2%) per day hit hote hi trading terminal band karo.
104. High-Impact News: NFP, CPI, FOMC, PPI ke time new entries avoid karo; wait for post-news 15m displacement.
